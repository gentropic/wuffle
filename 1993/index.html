    What this is

    StarStereo 93's Professional DOS Engine: a stereonet program for 32-bit DOS, with menus, a data table, a cursor to read the net with, Kamb contours, the orientation tensor and Woodcock's K, PCX output and a batch mode. It runs here in DOSBox, at the speed of the CPU on the case, with nothing fetched from anywhere but this site.

    Its arithmetic is integers only: CORDIC for every sine and arctangent, fixed point for the rest, no floating-point instruction anywhere in the program. The build checks that, and checks its numbers against wuffle's: every contour count agrees exactly.

    A design fiction

    StarStereo never existed. It is wuffle's imagined ancestor, written in 2026 as if it had shipped in 1993: C89, Open Watcom, the DOS/32A extender, Mode X. The real line it nods to is OpenStereo (Grohmann & Campanha, 2009/10) and its 2016 rewrite.
