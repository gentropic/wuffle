What this is

STARSTER is a stereonet program for 32-bit DOS: equal-area and equal-angle nets, poles and great circles, Kamb contours, the orientation tensor and Woodcock's K, PCX output and a batch mode. It runs here in DOSBox, in your browser, with nothing fetched from anywhere but this site.

Its arithmetic is integers only: CORDIC for every sine and arctangent, fixed point for the rest, no floating-point instruction anywhere in the program. The build checks that, and checks its numbers against wuffle's: every contour count agrees exactly.

A design fiction

StarStereo never existed. STARSTER is wuffle's imagined ancestor, written in 2026 as if it had shipped in 1993: C89, Open Watcom, the DOS/32A extender, Mode X. The real line it nods to is OpenStereo (Grohmann & Campanha, 2009/10) and its 2016 rewrite.

Your data

Drop a CSV from wuffle (a field log, or a dataset exported from the desk) on the screen: it is written to the C: drive as FIELD.DAT and STARSTER opens it. Plain text works too, dipdir dip a line.

At the DOS prompt: STARSTER /X FIELD94.DAT, TYPE README.TXT, DIR.
